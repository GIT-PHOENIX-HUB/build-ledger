## Troubleshooting Low Fuel Pressure

- If the pressure is too high, the solenoid will not open and the generator will not start.
- If the pressure is too low, the generator will be starved for fuel and will not start or will not run smoothly or be able to support a load.

### Common Causes

In most cases, a dealer or installer will need to address these issues: [Where Can I Find a Generac Dealer in the U.S. Or Canada?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Can-I-Find-a-Generac-Service-Dealer-in-My-Area)

- The liquid propane tank pressure is inadequate - [How Do I Determine the Proper Fuel Pressure for My Air-Cooled Home Standby Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Determine-the-Proper-Fuel-Pressure-for-My-Air-Cooled-Home-Standby-Generator)

- The natural gas service or meter is inadequate - [How Do I Determine the Proper Fuel Pressure for My Air-Cooled Home Standby Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Determine-the-Proper-Fuel-Pressure-for-My-Air-Cooled-Home-Standby-Generator)

- The pressure regulator is not set or sized correctly - This should be done by the dealer, installer, or gas company.
- The fuel line is not properly sized - [How Do I Determine the Fuel Line Sizing for My Air-Cooled Home Standby Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Determine-the-Fuel-Line-Sizing-for-My-Air-Cooled-Home-Standby-Generator)

Proper fuel flow is critical for generator operation under full load. Incorrect fuel pressure, undersized lines, or improper fuel conversions are not covered under warranty.
