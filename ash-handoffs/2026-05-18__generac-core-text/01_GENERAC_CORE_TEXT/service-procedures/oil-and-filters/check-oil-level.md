# Checking the oil level

A home standby generator's oil level should be checked frequently, especially when running continuously during an outage. Maintaining appropriate oil levels ensures the generator's engine does not suffer engine damage.

## Environment

These instructions apply to **Guardian series** home standby generators and the **Next Generation Air-Cooled Standby Generator**.

## Sections

- [Checking the oil on the Next Generation Air-Cooled Standby Generator](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Check-the-Oil-Level-in-My-Home-Standby-Generator#nextgen)
- [Checking the oil on Guardian-series generators](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Check-the-Oil-Level-in-My-Home-Standby-Generator#gurad)

## Checking the oil level on the Next Generation Air-Cooled Standby Generator

Follow the steps below to check the oil level:

1. Set the utility MLCB to **OFF**(OPEN).

**Note**: This is the first switch after the meter. For service entry-rated switches, this will be in the automatic transfer switch. For non-service-entry rated switches, this will be in the main circuit breaker for the home or in a standalone box.
2. Unlock and lift the lid.
3. Set the generator MLCB (generator disconnect) to **OFF**(OPEN).



![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMU100000HtoX5)
4. If the generator was running during the outage, allow it to cool down for approximately **one minute before pressing OFF.**
5. Press the **OFF** button to shut down the generator. **Wait five minutes before continuing.**
6. Remove the oil dipstick and wipe it dry with a clean cloth.

_22 kW - 28 kW dipstick location_

![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMU100000HtiY9)

_14 kW - 18 kW oil fill cap/dipstick location_

_![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMU100000HtiEm)_

_10 kW oil fill cap/dipstick location_

![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMU100000HtvVJ)

7. Completely insert the oil dipstick into the dipstick tube and remove it.
8. Observe the oil level. It should be at the **FULL** mark on the dipstick.
9. If needed, remove the oil fill cap and add the recommended oil until the oil level reaches the **FULL** mark. Reinsert the dipstick and install the fill cap. See: [What type of oil should I use in my home backup generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Type-of-Oil-Should-I-Use-in-My-Home-Backup-Generator)

### To restart the generator

1. Unlock and lift the lid.
2. Press the AUTO button on the control panel.
3. Allow the generator to start and warm up for a few minutes.
4. Set the generator MLCB (generator disconnect) to ON (CLOSED).

The system is now operating in AUTO mode. The utility MLCB can be turned ON (CLOSED).

### Checking the oil level on Guardian-series generators

**Important:** Verify the oil level daily when running the generator for extended periods during an outage. The generator will shut down if the oil level is low. If your generator is powering your house during an outage, it must be properly shut down before checking oil levels: [How Do I Shut Down My Generator When It’s Powering My House (During an Outage)?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Shut-Down-My-Generator-When-It-s-Powering-My-House)

1\. Set the utility main line circuit breaker switch (MLCB) to **OFF**(OPEN).

> **Note**: This is the first switch after the meter. For service entry-rated switches, this will be in the automatic transfer switch. For non-service-entry rated switches, this will be in the main circuit breaker for the home or in a standalone box.

2\. Set the generator MLCB (B) to **OFF** (OPEN).

![image.png](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EM5f000004XSwA)

**3\. IMPORTANT: If the generator was running during an outage: After flipping the breaker to remove loads from the generator, allow the generator to continue running without loads for a minimum of 5 minutes, then allow the generator to cool for an additional 5 minutes. Immediate shutdown after running under load can cause damage to engine components.**

4\. Press the red **OFF** button on the generator's control panel to turn the generator off. Wait five minutes.

5\. Remove the oil dipstick (F) and wipe it dry with a clean cloth.

![image.png](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EM5f000004XStp)

6\. Completely insert the oil dipstick into oil the dip-stick tube and remove it.

> **Note**: On generators featuring an 816cc engine (some 13 kW, 14 kW, 16 kW, and 18 kW models) the dipstick screws in. On these models, the dipstick should be screwed in fully when checking the oil level.

7\. Observe the oil level. The oil level should be somewhere between the red marks in the image below, up to the FULL mark on the oil dipstick.

![oil level.png](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMU1000009VERJ)

8\. If the oil level is at or below the **ADD** indicator, remove the oil fill cap (pictured below) and add small amounts of oil to the engine (with the oil dipstick removed) until the oil level reaches the **FULL** mark.

![IMG_0165-min (1).jpg](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000008jkj&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMU1000009VFOz)

> **Note**: See the [What Type of Oil Should I Use in My Home Backup Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Type-of-Oil-Should-I-Use-in-My-Home-Backup-Generator) article for information about choosing an appropriate oil.

9\. Insert and fully secure the dipstick, and completely tighten the oil fill cap. **IMPORTANT: An oil spill may occur If these elements are not secure.**

### To restart the generator:

1\. Press the **AUTO** button on the control panel.

2\. Allow the generator to start and warm up for a few minutes.

3\. Set the generator MLCB (generator disconnect) to **ON**(CLOSED).

4\. Now that the system is operating in AUTO, the utility MLCB can be turned **ON**(CLOSED).
