# Maintenance timer vs. maintenance reminder

Generac standby generators use both an internal **maintenance timer** and a **maintenance reminder**. The timer tracks operating hours to determine when service is required. The reminder is a yellow visual alert indicating that a maintenance interval has been reached and maintenance is due, but it does not stop the generator from running. For model-specific details about the yellow maintenance reminder light and how to reset it, see: [How do I clear a yellow maintenance reminder or warning on my standby generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Does-a-Shining-Yellow-Led-Light-Mean-on-the-Control-Panel-of-My-Generator)

## Environment

This article applies to Generac **air and liquid-cooled home standby generators**.

## Maintenance timer

The maintenance timer tracks the unit's operational hours to alert the owner when a predefined minimum maintenance interval has been reached. Intervals may vary depending on the type of unit and are outlined in the [maintenance schedule](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-Can-I-Find-the-Maintenance-Schedule-for-My-Generac-Home-Standby-Generator) of the owner's manual.

- **Automatic tracking**: The timer monitors hours and run time automatically.
- **Interval scheduling**: Maintenance intervals vary by unit. Refer to the maintenance schedule in your owner’s manual or see here: [Where Can I Find the Maintenance Schedule for My Generac Home Standby Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-Can-I-Find-the-Maintenance-Schedule-for-My-Generac-Home-Standby-Generator)
- **Automatic reset:** The timer resets automatically when the yellow maintenance reminder light is cleared. No user action is required.
- **Preemptive maintenance:** Maintenance may be done earlier if needed (for example, in dusty or extreme conditions). Keep detailed records of all maintenance performed. For more details, see here: [Viewing, Adding, Deleting, & Exporting Maintenance Schedule Records in Mobile Link](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Viewing-Adding-Deleting-Exporting-Maintenance-Schedule-Records-in-Mobile-Link)

## Maintenance reminder

The maintenance reminder serves as a reminder when scheduled maintenance is due, based on the maintenance timer.

- **Visual alert:** A yellow LED light indicates that a maintenance interval has been reached.

- **Reminder schedule:** Maintenance intervals are based on the generator’s total run hours and the time elapsed since the last maintenance reminder was cleared, whichever occurs first.
- **Function:** The reminder alerts users that maintenance is due and helps ensure the [maintenance schedule](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-Can-I-Find-the-Maintenance-Schedule-for-My-Generac-Home-Standby-Generator)is followed.
- **Impact on operation:** The reminder does not stop the generator from running.
- **Resetting the reminder**: For model-specific details about the yellow maintenance reminder light and how to reset it, see: [How do I clear a yellow maintenance reminder or warning on my standby generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Does-a-Shining-Yellow-Led-Light-Mean-on-the-Control-Panel-of-My-Generator)

## Maintenance best practices

- **Dealer support**: Generac recommends using a Generac Independent Authorized Service Dealer (IASD) for maintenance: [How Can I Find a Generac Service Dealer in My Area?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Can-I-Find-a-Generac-Service-Dealer-in-My-Area)
- **Follow the [maintenance schedule](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-Can-I-Find-the-Maintenance-Schedule-for-My-Generac-Home-Standby-Generator)**: At a minimum, always follow the maintenance intervals listed in the owner’s manual: [Where can I find a user manual or other documentation for my home standby generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Find-a-Copy-of-My-Home-Standby-Generator-Owner-s-Manual-and-Spec-Sheets)
- **Be proactive:** Inspections and early maintenance can help protect your investment and ensure reliability.
