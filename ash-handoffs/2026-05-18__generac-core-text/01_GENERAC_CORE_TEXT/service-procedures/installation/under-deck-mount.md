## Can I mount a home standby generator under a deck?

## No, DO NOT install under wooden decks or structures. This article briefly answers whether it is permitted to install standby generators under a deck or overhang.      - Mar 16, 2026 - Knowledge

### Fields

Title

Can I mount a home standby generator under a deck?

### Content

Public Answer

Home standby generators should not be installed under decks, overhangs, or overhead structures of any kind. See the below resources regarding generator clearance requirements:

- [What Should I Know About Clearance Requirements When Installing My Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Should-I-Know-About-Clearance-Requirements-When-Installing-My-Generator)
- [What Is NFPA 37?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Is-NFPA-37)
- [What Does the "SwRI" Logo Mean on My Home Standby Generator Housing?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Does-the-SwRI-Logo-Mean-on-My-Home-Standby-Generator-Housing)
