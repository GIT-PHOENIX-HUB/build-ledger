# Installation Costs and Permits -- Residential Generac

**Source:** Compiled from Generac and industry installation resources, scraped 2026-04-14.

## Typical Installation Cost Range

**Total installed cost (generator + installation):** $8,000 - $16,000+

This range includes the generator unit itself plus labor, materials, permits,
and all electrical/gas work.

### Cost Breakdown

| Component | Typical Cost |
|-----------|-------------|
| Generator unit (MSRP) | $3,649 - $9,509 |
| Transfer switch (if not bundled) | $500 - $1,200 |
| Electrical labor | $1,500 - $3,500 |
| Concrete pad | $200 - $600 |
| Gas line work (plumber) | $500 - $1,500 |
| Permits | $100 - $500 |
| Miscellaneous materials | $200 - $500 |

### Factors That Affect Cost

- **Generator size** -- Larger kW units cost more for both equipment and installation.
- **Transfer switch type** -- Whole-house smart switch costs more than 16-circuit.
- **Gas line distance** -- Longer runs from the meter increase plumbing costs.
- **Panel condition** -- Older panels may need upgrading.
- **Location/access** -- Difficult access, slopes, or tight spaces increase labor.
- **Local permit fees** -- Vary by municipality from $50 to $500+.

## Permits Required

Most jurisdictions require permits for standby generator installation:

1. **Electrical permit** -- Required in nearly all areas. Covers the transfer
   switch installation, generator wiring, and panel connections.
2. **Plumbing/mechanical permit** -- Required for natural gas or propane line work.
3. **Building permit** -- Some jurisdictions require this for the concrete pad
   or if structural modifications are needed.

### Permit Process

1. Submit application with site plan showing generator location, clearances,
   and gas line routing.
2. Pay permit fees ($100-$500 depending on jurisdiction).
3. Install the generator.
4. Schedule and pass inspection(s) -- typically electrical and gas/mechanical.

### Clearance Requirements

- **18 inches minimum** from the structure (Generac spec for most models).
- **5 feet** from any opening window, door, or fresh air intake.
- **Away from** HVAC condensers, dryer vents, and combustible materials.
- Check local codes -- some jurisdictions have stricter setback requirements.

## Installation Timeline

| Phase | Typical Duration |
|-------|-----------------|
| Site survey and quote | 1-2 hours |
| Permit application | 1-3 weeks (varies by jurisdiction) |
| Equipment delivery | 1-2 weeks after order |
| Installation day | 1 day (8-10 hours typical) |
| Inspection | 1-2 weeks after installation |
| Total start to finish | 3-6 weeks |
