## Common Features (All Current Guardian Models)

- **True Power Technology** -- Less than 5% Total Harmonic Distortion (THD)
- **Evolution Controller** -- Multilingual LCD display (EN/ES/FR/PT)
- **Mobile Link WiFi** -- FREE remote monitoring included
- **Quiet-Test Mode** -- Weekly self-test at reduced RPM
- **Fuel Flexibility** -- Natural gas or LP operation (dual-fuel)
- **Aluminum Enclosure** -- Bisque color, corrosion resistant
- **Warranty** -- 5-year limited
- **UL/cUL Listed** -- For installation as close as 18 inches to a structure

## Natural Gas vs. LP Ratings

Natural gas output is typically 10-15% lower than LP ratings. The kW rating
on the model number is the LP rating. For example, the 26kW (7290) produces
26,000W on LP but approximately 22,500W on natural gas.

Always size based on the fuel type the home will actually use.

---

## Discontinued Models Referenced in Older Listings

Generac has historically offered 7.5kW, 9kW, 11kW, 13kW, 16kW, and 20kW
models. These are discontinued but may still be encountered in the field
for service. The current (2025/2026) air-cooled lineup is:
10kW, 14kW, 18kW, 22kW, 24kW, 26kW, 28kW.

The 15kW EcoGen (7163) is a specialty off-grid model, not part of the
standard Guardian series.
