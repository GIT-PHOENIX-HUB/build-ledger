# Can the pickup and dropout voltage for a standby generator be adjusted?

Standby generator systems monitor utility voltage and use two voltage thresholds, called dropout and pickup, to decide when to switch power.

## What do “dropout,” “pickup,” and “power transfer” mean?

- **Dropout voltage:** Utility power is too low, so the system starts the generator and switches the home to **generator** power.
- **Pickup voltage:** Utility power has returned to an acceptable level, so the system can switch the home back to **utility** power.
- **Power transfer:** The automatic transfer switch (ATS) changes the [home's power source](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-is-a-standby-generator) from utility power to generator power, or back again.

## Can these settings be adjusted?

**Yes.** On supported systems, a Generac independent authorized service dealer can adjust the pickup and dropout voltage. These are not settings homeowners can change.

To find a dealer, use the [dealer locator](https://www.generac.com/dealer-locator/).

## Can power transfer be prevented for brown outs and power flashes?

[Brown outs](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Is-a-Brown-out-Kit-and-How-Does-It-Work) are brief drops in utility voltage (not a full outage). Power flashes are very short outages that quickly return.

- **Next Generation Series generators:** include built-in brownout protection to help reduce unwanted switching when utility power is unstable.
- **Startup delay:** In some cases, a [dealer](https://www.generac.com/dealer-locator/) can adjust the startup delay, so the generator does not start for very short outages. Learn more: [Can the Startup Delay Time for a Home Standby Generator Be Adjusted](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Can-the-Startup-Delay-Time-for-a-Home-Standby-Generator-Be-Adjusted).
- **Brownout kits (Discontinued):** Guardian Series and older systems used this add-on. Learn more: [What Is a Brown-out Kit and How Does It Work](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Is-a-Brown-out-Kit-and-How-Does-It-Work).
