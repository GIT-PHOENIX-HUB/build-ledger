# Generac generator accessories

Generac offers a variety of accessories for your portable and home standby generator to help you get the most out of your investment.

> **Note**: This article only offers **a summary overview** of commonly requested details regarding generator available accessories and **is not exhaustive or guaranteed to be completely accurate and up to date**. View your generator's specifications sheet using the links below to view available accessories for your specific model and verify compatibility. Please verify product details before purchasing.

## Environment

This article applies to **most portable generators**, **air-cooled home standby generators**, and **residential liquid-cooled generators**.

> **Note:** Accessories are optional add-ons, while parts are required for safe generator operation, such as replacement parts. For parts information, see:

- [Where Can I Purchase Parts for My Portable Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-Can-I-Purchase-Parts-for-My-Portable-Generator)
- [Where can I source parts for my home standby generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/I-Don-t-Need-a-Complete-Maintenance-Kit-Right-Now-Where-Can-I-Buy-Individual-Parts)

### Where can I purchase Generac generator accessories?

Accessories can be purchased by visiting [www.generac.com](https://www.generac.com/), [Generac's OrderTree page](https://generac.ordertree.com/), or contacting [your local Generac dealer](https://www.generac.com/dealer-locator/).

- [How Can I Find a Generac Service Dealer in My Area?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Can-I-Find-a-Generac-Service-Dealer-in-My-Area)

### How do I find part numbers for compatible Generac accessories for my generator's specific model?

Refer to your **owner's manual** and **specification sheet** to find part numbers and verify accessory compatibility. Product documentation, including owner's manuals and specifications sheets, is available at the following link with your generator's serial number.

- [Product information and documentation](https://www.generac.com/support/product-info-user-manuals/)

### Do I need my serial number/model number?

Yes, some accessories vary by model and are only compatible with specific units. Using your serial/model number, you can find the correct documentation with the appropriate accessory part number. Your serial/model number is required to identify the correct parts and verify compatibility. See: [Where Can I Find the Serial Number on My Air or Liquid-Cooled Standby Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-Do-I-Find-the-Serial-Number-on-My-Air-or-Liquid-Cooled-Standby-Generator)

> **Note**: For items requiring wiring for installation (e.g., manual transfer switch, power inlet box, Generac Load managers, etc.), consult your local Generac Independent Authorized Service Dealer or qualified professional and follow local and state codes and regulations.

## Portable generator accessories

**[Maintenance Kits (link)](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/portable-power-solutions/aftermarket--parts/0198280-a-portable-maint-kits.pdf)**

- Schedule maintenance is a necessity for all portable generators. The portable maintenance kits offer all the components necessary to perform complete maintenance on Generac portable generators. Oil will need to be purchased separately based on the recommendations listed. Maintenance is recommended seasonally but may differ per model. More frequent service is required when operating in adverse conditions. Refer to the generator owner's manual for more information.
- Items included (if applicable).
  - Air filter, oil filter, spark plug(s), oil funnel, chamois, Stabil sample, spark plug wrench.

> **Note**: Contents of each maintenance kit vary by model. Check your user manual to verify which maintenance kit you should buy, or reach out to your local service dealer.

[**Manual Transfer Switch (link)**](https://www.generac.com/residential-products/portable-generator-transfer-switches/)

- Manual Transfer Switches allow you to connect your portable generator to hardwired appliances. Ensure essential items like your furnace, sump pump, or lights are backed up by your portable generator during an outage.

**[Power Inlet Boxes (link)](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/portable-power-solutions/aftermarket--parts/0196860-a-portable-power-inlet-boxes.pdf)**

- Power Inlet Boxes (PIB) allow owners to safely connect their portable generator to a manual transfer switch. When the power goes out, they simply plug one end of a power cord into their generator and the other into the PIB.

**[Generator Plugs and Connectors (link)](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/portable-power-solutions/aftermarket--parts/0196880-a-plugs-and-connectors.pdf)**

**[Extension Cords (link)](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/portable-power-solutions/aftermarket--parts/power-cords-spec-sheet_a0000001964.pdf)**

- Heavy duty generator extension cords with multiple connection ports for generators with 20 and 30 Amp outlets.
- Generator cords for manual transfer switch/ power inlet box connections.

**Optional Accessories**

- [Switched Neutral Kit](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/portable-power-solutions/aftermarket--parts/0195040-b-gentran-switches-model-6924-6925-6926.pdf)
- [Flush Mounting Plate](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/portable-power-solutions/aftermarket--parts/0196870-a-flush-mounting-plate-spec.pdf)
- [Power Inlet Box Flip Lid](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/portable-power-solutions/aftermarket--parts/500514b-pib-flip-lid-manual.pdf) -This optional flip lid is intended for applications where covering the power inlet is desired, such as keeping out nesting insects, dirt, moisture, etc. (The Power Inlet Box is sold separately).

### General air-cooled home standby generator accessories

[Check you user manual](https://www.generac.com/support/product-info-user-manuals/) to verify accessories that are compatible with your specific generator model.

#### Batteries

Battery selection depends on your generator model, and in some cases, the controller. [See this article for more information](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Battery-Do-I-Need-for-My-Home-Standby-Generator).

**26R Wet Cell Battery**

- This battery is used by Guardian series and older generator models.

**35AGM Battery**

- This battery is an alternative recommendation to the standard 26R. This is a maintenance-free style battery.

**BTX20L AGM Battery**

This is the battery required by the Next Generation Air-Cooled Standby Generator.

#### Cold Weather Accessories

- **Battery Pad Warmer**


  - The pad warmer rests under the battery. It is recommended for use if the temperature regularly falls below 0°F (-18°C).
- **Breather Warmer**
  - The breather warmer is for use in extreme cold weather applications. This is for use with Evolution controllers only in climates where heavy icing occurs.
- **Oil warmer**
  - The oil warmer slips directly over the oil filter. It is recommended for use if the temperature regularly falls before 0°F (-18°C).
- See the following article on [cold weather accessories](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-is-a-Generac-Cold-Weather-Kit) for more detailed information on components and compatibility.

**Auxiliary Transfer Switch Contact Kit**

- The auxiliary transfer switch contact kit allows the transfer switch to lock out a single large electrical load you may not need. It is not compatible with 50 amp pre-wired switches.

**Fascia Base Wrap Kit**

- The fascia base wrap snaps together around the bottom of Guardian-series air-cooled generators. It offers a sleek, contoured appearance and helps protect against rodents and insects by covering the lifting holes in the base. This accessory is not required by the Next Generation Air-Cooled Standby Generator.

**Touch-up Paint Kit**

- If the generator enclosure is scratched or damaged, it is important to touch up the paint to protect it from future corrosion. The touch-up paint kit includes the necessary paint to correctly maintain or touch up a generator enclosure.

**Schedule maintenance Kit**

- Generac's scheduled maintenance kits provide all the items necessary to perform complete routine maintenance on a Generac automatic standby generator (oil not included).

**Propane Monitoring Accessory**

- The cellular-enabled LP fuel level monitor provides constant monitoring of the connected LP fuel tank. Monitoring the LP tank's fuel level is an important step in verifying the generator is ready to run during an unexpected power failure. Status alerts are available through the Mobile Link application to notify users when the LP tank is in need of a refill.
- [Propane Monitoring Accessory](https://www.generac.com/services-applications/propane-monitoring/ "Propane Monitoring Accessory")

**Mobile Link accessories**

**Generac Load Managers**

- Generac load managers optimize the performance of a standby generator. They manage large electrical loads upon startup and shed them to aid in recovery when overloaded. In many cases, using the Generac Load Manager can reduce the overall size and cost of the system.
- For more information see the following article, [What is a Generac Load Manager (previously known as a Smart Management Module (SMM))?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Is-a-Smart-Management-Module-SMM)

## Liquid-cooled home standby generator

For Liquid-Cooled home standby generators, referring to owner's manuals, specifications sheets and consulting with your local Generac IASD is especially important, as some accessories are specialized for specific units and compatibility varies. Some accessories may require additional components, and all accessories must comply with local and state codes and regulation. This information is for reference only - contract your nearest Generac IASD for specific part numbers and compatibility details.

### Three-phase XG Generator Model Kits

- **3-Phase voltage configuration kits**
- **3-Pole circuit breaker kits**

### Control System Kits

- **Generator Ready-status Indicator Kit** \- 3-Color LED providing at-a-glace indication of generator ready-to-run status; mounts to frount of generator.
- **Enclosure Mounted Emergency Stop Kit**
- **Remote Emergency Stop Kit** \- (Surface Mount, Flush Mount, and Break Glass)
- **Generac Load Mangers (50 Amp and 100 Amp)**
  - Generac load managers optimize the performance of a standby generator. They manage large electrical loads upon startup and shed them to aid in recovery when overloaded. In many cases, using the Generac Load Manager can reduce the overall size and cost of the system.
  - For more information see the following article, [What is a Generac Load Manager (previously known as a Smart Management Module (SMM))?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Is-a-Smart-Management-Module-SMM)
- **Propane Monitoring Accessory** - The cellular-enabled LP fuel level monitor provides constant monitoring of the connected LP fuel tank. Monitoring the LP tank's fuel level is an important step in verifying the generator is ready to run during an unexpected power failure. Status alerts are available through the Mobile Link application to notify users when the LP tank is in need of a refill.
  - [Propane Monitoring Accessory](https://www.generac.com/services-applications/propane-monitoring/ "Propane Monitoring Accessory")

### Power Zone Kits

- **NFPA 110 Controller Kit** \- Includes controller module with Key Switch, Alarm Horn, and Emergency Stop Switch which connects to Power Zone 410 controller; additional components required  to be considered for NFPA 110 system control and remote annunciation.
  - Contract your local generator IASD for more information.
- **Remote Relay Panel** \- Remote relay panel without LEDs or keypad; mounts in the structure that is connected to backup power.
- **Remote Annunciator Panel with 8 Relays** \- Remote annunciator panel with relays; mounts in teh structure that is connected to backup power.
- **Remote Annunciator without relays** \- Remote annunciator panel without relays; mounts in the structure that is connected to backup power.
- **Power Zone 410 I/O Extender Kit** \- Expands I/O for the Power Zone 410 controller to provide connections for additional accessories; connects to controller with a 3 wire RS-485 interface.
- **Power Zone Gateway Kit** \- Provides an Ethernet connection port to the generator for a Building Management System (BMS); **Not intended for or able to be used with Mobile Link.**

### Operating Environment Kits

- **Battery Heater Kit**\- Recommended for operating environments where the temperature drops below 32°F (0°C); externally powered by 120 VAC, 60 Hz.
- **Engine Block Heater Kit** \- Recommended for operating environments where the temperature drops below 0°F (-18°C) externally powered by 120 VAC, 60 Hz.
- **Level 2 Sound Reduction Kit** \- Further reduces sound level of generator directing exhaust to the side; assembles to top of generator enclosure exhaust discharge area.
- **Extreme High Wind Kit** \- Increases wind speed rating of generator to 186 mph (300 km/h); assembles to exterior of generator enclosure and frame.

### Installation Kits

- **Rooftop & Elevated Mounting Sub-Baseframe Structure Kit** \- Support structure for mounting a generator on a rooftop or elevated frame; **does NOT include the frame itself**.
- **Baseframe Block-off Kit** \- Aluminum Panel to close off the bottom of the generator; provides sufficient airflow through the generator while keeping objects out; **required** whenever the **generator is elevated**.
- **Engine Fluid Containment Kit** \- Containment pan to capture all engine oil and coolant; includes a sensor to detect the presence of fluid in the pan and then displays a warning on the controller screen; **requires Power Zone 410 I/O Extender kit for sensor installation.**
- **Seismic Anchoring Kit** \- Anchor bolts for securing the generator in a defined, seismically active area with recommended mounting surface recommendations; **does not supersede local codes or requirements**; always follow local codes and regulations for seismic requirements.
- **Base Plug Kit** \- Base plugs to fit in the lifting holes of the baseframe to keep debris out.

### Maintenance Kits

- **Scheduled and Engine Maintenance Kits -** The liquid-cooled schedule maintenance kits offer all the hardware necessary to perform complete maintenance on Generac liquid-cooled generators.
- **Touch-up Paint Kits -** If the generator enclosure is scratched or damaged, it is important to touch up the paint to protect it from future corrosion. The touch-up paint kit includes the necessary paint to correctly maintain or touch up a generator enclosure.
- **Ultrasonic Cleaner Solution** \- An ultra-concentrated anti-corrosive cleaning solution engineered to reach the smallest cavities to clean the toughest contaminants. This **water based** formula is **non-toxic, biodegradable, safe for both metal and plastic surfaces**, and is superior in rinsability.
- **All Surface Protectant** \- All surface protectant for vinyl, rubber, plastics creates a barrier that seals and protects surfaces from water, UV rays while renewing the look of the surface.

### Transfer Switch Kits

- **3-Phase Voltage Sensing Kit** \- 3 Phase Voltage Sensing Kit **required** for RTS Transfer Switches **when used with** the Power Zone 410 controller for 3-Phase voltage.
- For more information contact your local Generac IASD.
