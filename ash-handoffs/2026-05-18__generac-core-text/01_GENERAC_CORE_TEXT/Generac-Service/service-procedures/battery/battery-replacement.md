# Selecting and replacing a battery for a home standby generator

- Home standby generators do not come with a battery, and it must be purchased separately.
- For specific battery size information, reference the generator's spec sheet or owner's manual on the [online product lookup page.](https://www.generac.com/support/product-info-user-manuals/)
- For additional information on choosing the correct battery for a home standby generator, see here: [What battery do I need for my home standby generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Battery-Do-I-Need-for-My-Home-Standby-Generator)
