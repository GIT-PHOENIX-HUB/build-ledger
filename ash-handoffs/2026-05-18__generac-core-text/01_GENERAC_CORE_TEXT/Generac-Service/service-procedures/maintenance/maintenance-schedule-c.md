# Schedule C Maintenance

Home standby generators require specific maintenance tasks to be completed at different intervals to ensure proper operation and longevity. **Schedule C maintenance** refers to a collection of tasks that should be performed promptly after the generator has accumulated 25 run hours, typically during the initial break-in period. Home standby generators are equipped with a yellow maintenance alert which will display on the generator's side status light when the Schedule C Maintenance interval has been reached. Completing Schedule C maintenance helps ensure the generator operates smoothly during its first critical hours of use. For more information about maintenance alerts, see [What is the difference between maintenance reminders, warnings, and alarms?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-is-the-difference-between-maintenance-reminders-warnings-and-alarms)

## Environment

This article applies to **Generac Next Generation Series** air-cooled generators during the Schedule C maintenance interval **(after the first 25 run hours** **).**

## Schedule C Maintenance Tasks

- Change Oil and Oil Filter - [How do I change the oil and oil filter in a Generac air-cooled standby generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Change-the-Oil-and-Oil-Filter-in-a-Generac-Air-Cooled-Home-Standby-Generator)

## Who can perform maintenance?

Homeowners can perform regular maintenance tasks as outlined in their generator's user manual. Alternatively, homeowners can contact a local certified service dealer. See[Where can I find a company to perform maintenance on my generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-Can-I-Find-a-Company-to-Perform-Standard-Maintenance-On-My-Generator) for more information.

## Reset the maintenance reminder and update the log

Regardless of who performs the service, maintenance records should be kept for future reference. The Mobile Link for Generators app can be used to record and track these tasks. When you clear a maintenance reminder in the app, it also resets the generator’s internal maintenance interval clock for recurring items. Always confirm that the maintenance log reflects the most recent activity. For details, see [Viewing, adding, deleting, & exporting maintenance schedule records in Mobile Link](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Viewing-Adding-Deleting-Exporting-Maintenance-Schedule-Records-in-Mobile-Link).

## Next steps

After maintenance procedures have been completed, it is important to follow the post-maintenance check procedure to ensure the home standby unit is "ready to run", as well as document/record the maintenance performed.
