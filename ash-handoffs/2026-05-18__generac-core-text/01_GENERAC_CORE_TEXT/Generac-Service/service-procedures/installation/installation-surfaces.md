# What surfaces can I install my home standby generator on?

When preparing a mounting surface for an air-cooled home standby generator, there are three commonly used and approved surfaces:

- Compacted, leveled pea gravel or crushed stone.
- Approved composite pad.
- Approved concrete base pad. We recommend GenPad- model numbers 10000007852 (3 in.) and 10000007848 (4 in.).

## Environment

This article applies to all air-cooled home standby generators.

All air-cooled home standby generators come with an integrated composite pad. This integrated composite pad elevates the generator and helps prevent water from pooling around the base.

![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0U10000006Yev&feoid=00N5f00000gWhDU&refid=0EMU100000AwQCT)

The integrated composite pad allows the generator to be placed on the surface types mentioned above. See local codes to verify if a base is required, and if so, what type. If a base pad is required, all federal, state, and local codes must be followed. For guidance on mounting a generator to a concrete base pad, see [What Are the Requirements for Mounting A Home Standby Generator to Concrete?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Are-the-Requirements-for-Mounting-A-Home-Standby-Generator-to-Concrete) for more information.

## Surface preparation

Prepare a rectangular area approximately 5 in. (127 mm) thick (A) and approximately 3 in. longer and wider (B) than the footprint of the generator on all sides when using pea gravel or crushed stone.

![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0U10000006Yev&feoid=00N5f00000gWhDU&refid=0EMU100000AwKLa)

_Note: The surface where the generator is installed must be level within 0.5 in. (13mm) all around._

## Placement on roofs, platforms, and other supporting structures

Where required to place generator on a roof, platform, deck, or other supporting structure, generator must be placed in accordance with the requirements in NFPA 37, Section 4.1.3. Surface beneath the generator and beyond must be noncombustible to a minimum distance of 12 inches. Contact local building inspection department or fire department to determine which noncombustible materials are approved for installation.

## Additional considerations

- It is important to select a non-combustible base type as required by local laws or codes.
- Ensure the surface where the generator is mounted is compacted, leveled, and will not erode over time.
- Concrete or composite base pads must be appropriately sized in accordance with national, state, or local building codes.
- We recommend generators be installed by Generac authorized service dealers. [Click here](https://www.generac.com/home-standby-generators/dealer-locator/) to find a service dealer near you.
