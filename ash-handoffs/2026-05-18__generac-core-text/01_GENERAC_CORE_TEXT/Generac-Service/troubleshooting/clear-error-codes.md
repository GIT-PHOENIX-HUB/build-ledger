# Resetting and clearing generator alarm codes

Generator alarm codes notify the owner when something needs to be addressed in order to ensure the proper functioning of the home standby system. Some alarm codes can simply be cleared after the problem is resolved, while other alarm codes will require a visit from a Generac authorized service dealer: [What Error Codes Do I Need to Call a Dealer For?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Error-Codes-Do-I-Need-to-Call-a-Dealer-For)

> **Note**: Alarms are different from maintenance reminders and warnings: [What is the difference between maintenance reminders, warnings, and alarms?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-is-the-difference-between-maintenance-reminders-warnings-and-alarms)

Generator owners should always consult the owner's manual or Mobile Link to uncover the cause of an error code. See [How Do I Find a Copy of My Home Standby Generator Owner's Manual and Spec Sheets?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Find-a-Copy-of-My-Home-Standby-Generator-Owner-s-Manual-and-Spec-Sheets) for help finding a specific generator's user manual. A list of alarm codes can also be found in the [Where can I find a list of error codes for my generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Where-can-I-find-a-list-of-error-codes-for-my-generator) article.

> **Note:** Generator owners often confuse the hours of protection for an error code (example: "45091 (H)" means 45091 hours of protection.) For more information, see the [What Does Hours of Protection mean on the Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-Does-Hours-of-Protection-mean-on-the-Generator) article.

## Next Generation series generators

## 1\. To clear an alarm code on a **Next Generation Series** generator with a **Power Zone 200** controller,hold the **OFF** button for at least three seconds. The red LED will become solid when the alarm is cleared.

![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000007aZp&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMU100000HqaDh)

2\. To return the generator to service, or 'ready to run', press the **AUTO** button on the control panel.

3\. If the alarm returns after setting the unit to **AUTO**, [contact a Generac authorized service dealer](https://www.generac.com/home-standby-generators/home-dealer-locator/) for help.

> **Note**: Users with a Premium Mobile Link subscription can clear **maintenance reminders** but not **warnings or alarms** using Mobile Link: [How to Dismiss a Warning on the Mobile Link Application and Website](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-to-Dismiss-a-Warning-on-the-Mobile-Link-Application-and-Website)

## Nexus controllers

## 1\. Move the rocker switch to **OFF**.

2. Press **Enter** twice.

3\. Move the rocker switch back to **AUTO**.

4\. If the alarm returns after setting the unit to **AUTO**, [contact a Generac authorized service dealer](https://www.generac.com/home-standby-generators/home-dealer-locator/) for help.

> **Note:** For Nexus controllers without the rocker switch, press the **OFF** button, then press enter twice to clear an alarm code.

## Liquid-cooled generators with a Power Zone 410 controller

Home standby generators equipped with a **Power Zone 410 controller** display alarm codes when a condition is detected that could affect safe operation. These alarms are designed to protect the generator and your home. Before attempting to clear an alarm, always identify and correct the underlying issue, such as low oil level, a discharged battery, or an engine overspeed condition. If you are unsure what an alarm or error code means, refer [to your owner’s manual.](https://www.generac.com/support/product-info-user-manuals/)

> **IMPORTANT:** Never clear an alarm without correcting the cause. Some alarms, including shutdowns related to engine protection or electrical faults, are not owner-resettable. Repeatedly clearing alarms without resolving the issue can lead to generator damage or unsafe operation.

Once the issue has been corrected and the generator is no longer in an active fault condition, many Power Zone 410 alarms can be cleared directly from the controller display. Using the keypad, navigate to the alarm screen. See below for a representation of the alarm screen:

![](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0US0000007aZp&feoid=00N5f00000hVqcE&refid=0EMUS00000LXvRf)

- Use the navigation arrow buttons **(⬆ / ⬇)** to scroll through active alarms.
- To clear all alarms currently present, press **STOP** and then **ENTER** while on the Alarm Screen to clear all alarms present

If the alarm clears successfully, the generator should return to normal operation. If the alarm immediately reappears, or if the generator will not start after clearing the alarm, **do not continue clearing the alarm.** This usually indicates a condition that requires professional diagnosis. In these cases, contact an authorized Generac service provider using the [dealer locator](https://www.generac.com/dealer-locator/).

> **Note:** Alarm codes and descriptions vary based on generator model, configuration, and detected conditions. Some messages indicate warnings or maintenance reminders, while others indicate faults that may prevent operation.
