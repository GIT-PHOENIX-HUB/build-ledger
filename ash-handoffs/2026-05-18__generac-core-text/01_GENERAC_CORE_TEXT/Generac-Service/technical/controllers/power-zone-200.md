# PowerZone 200 control panel overview

**PowerZone 200 Control Panel**

![PowerZone 200 Control Panel](https://support.generac.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0U10000008egD&feoid=00N5f00000gWhDU&refid=0EMU100000EA8Yv)

Introduced in April 2025, the Power Zone 200 controller is available on air-cooled generator models. It replaces the Evolution 2.0 controller and adds expanded remote control capabilities.

## How to identify this controller in the field

**Look at the front of the controller panel:**
- **No digital display or screen** → Power Zone 200. Errors are only visible in the Mobile Link or Field Pro app.
- **Has an LCD/digital display** → Evolution or Nexus controller. Not a Power Zone 200.

This distinction matters for error code routing. Some codes (e.g., 3204) are Power Zone 200-only and should not appear on Evolution units. When in doubt, check for the presence or absence of a display panel first.

## Which generators use this controller?

The Power Zone 200 controller is used on Next Generation Air-Cooled Standby generators.

## What is the purpose of the Power Zone 200 controller?

The Power Zone 200 serves as the generator’s "brain", handling several key functions:

- Utility sensing (loss and reconnection)
- Starting and stopping the generator
- Power transfer
- Voltage regulation
- Exercise scheduling
- Battery charging

## Physical differences

Unlike the Evolution and Nexus panels, which included a digital display, the PowerZone 200 does not have a screen.

## Functionality

- Service mode: [How to place a generator into service mode](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-to-Place-My-Generator-into-Service-Mode)
- Power transfer during exercise: [Using the "Exercise Now" and "Exercise now and transfer power" commands](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Using-the-Exercise-Now-command-in-Mobile-Link)
- Adjustable extended exercise time: [Can I change the exercise cycle duration?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Can-I-Change-the-Exercise-Cycle-Duration)

## Operating modes: Auto, Manual, Off, and Service

- **Auto:** Automatically starts and powers the home when utility power is lost.
- **Manual:** Starts the engine but does not transfer power unless utility service fails.
- **Off:** The generator will not start, even if utility power is lost.
- **Service mode:** Allows the generator to remain off for maintenance or testing. Suppresses alerts and notifications during this time.

## Connectivity options

The PowerZone 200 is compatible with the [Cellular Connectivity device](https://generac.ordertree.com/cellular-kit-12v-ac-lc/5638937076.p).

## Applications

The primary tool for remote monitoring is the [Mobile Link application](https://support.generac.com/mobilelink/s/). The Field Pro app also provides [basic features for end users and advanced tools for technicians](https://support.generac.com/s/article/Permission-Levels-in-Field-Pro).
