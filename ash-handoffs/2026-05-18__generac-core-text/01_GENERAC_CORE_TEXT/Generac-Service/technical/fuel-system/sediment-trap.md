# Integrated sediment trap

Beginning with the 2016 model year, Guardian series generators include a factory-integrated sediment trap. This feature is also standard on Next Generation series generators.

## Environment

This article applies to:

- Guardian series generators manufactured in 2016 and later
- Next Generation series generators

## Sediment trap details

- Designed to prevent unwanted particles or debris from entering the fuel regulator and engine.
- Factory-integrated on each unit, eliminating the need for installers to build one in the field.
- Ensures all units have a properly built sediment trap.
- Saves installation time and materials.
- The sediment trap should be cleaned as part of regular maintenance. For steps, see: [How Do I Clean the Sediment Trap in a Generac Air-Cooled Home Standby Generator?](https://support.generac.com/s/article/How-Do-I-Clean-the-Sediment-Trap-in-a-Generac-Air-Cooled-Home-Standby-Generator)
