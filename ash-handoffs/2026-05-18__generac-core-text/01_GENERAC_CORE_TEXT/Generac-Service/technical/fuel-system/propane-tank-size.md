# Propane tank minimum size

Propane tanks supplying liquid propane vapor to a Generac home standby generator must be at least **250 gallons**, and larger tank capacity may be needed depending on the installation, fuel demand, or climate. A [free in-home estimate](https://support.generac.com/s/article/What-is-a-Generac-In-Home-Consultation-IHC) will help determine the best setup tailored to a specific home or site. In-home consultations are available at no charge, with no obligation to proceed with installation.

## Environment

This article applies to:

- Dual-fuel (NG, LP) residential standby generators
- Liquid-propane residential standby generators

## Fuel tank requirements

Correct tank sizing supports:

- Adequate fuel pressure for proper generator operation.
- Sufficient runtime in case fuel delivery is delayed or temporarily unavailable.
