# Reliable generator monitoring withMobile Link

Get real-time generator alerts, manage your exercise schedule, connect to a dealer, and more! Download the Mobile Link app and start monitoring your generator today.

[Sign Up for Mobile Link](https://app.mobilelinkgen.com/)

1. [Home](https://www.generac.com/)
2. [Home Standby Generators](https://www.generac.com/home-standby-generators/)
3. WiFi Remote Monitoring

## Stay connected to your generator anytime, anywhere

Get total peace of mind over your home energy backup system, right from your pocket. With Mobile Link, you can stay connected to your standby generator and propane supply 24/7. Real-time data, automatic notifications, and proactive maintenance reminders help ensure your system is always ready when you need it most.

### Stay informed with real-time generator status alerts

Receive up-to-the-minute text, email, and push notifications directly to your smart device to ensure you’re always aware of your generator’s status, whether it’s running, exercising, in need of attention, or ready to go!

![girl smiling down at phone in the dark with a lamp on ](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/home-standby-generators/home-standby-generators/mobile-link/girl-on-phone-sm.png)

### Ensure reliable power with run time monitoring

Monitoring your generator’s run time and managing exercise schedules through the app ensures maintenance is performed on time, protecting your warranty and preventing costly repairs. This helps maximize reliability, avoid unexpected downtime, and extend the life of your generator.

![Mobile link by generac app on a phone](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/home-standby-generators/home-standby-generators/mobile-link/mobile-link-action.png)

### Stay connected to your preferred dealer or request service, all from one location

Easily select your preferred dealer within the Mobile Link app for easy access to their contact information. You can also send service requests to a dealer in your area in just a few taps - no phone calls or emails required.

![A generac installer smiling next to a newly installed generator](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/home-standby-generators/application_image_houstonyellowstonephotoshoot10_2025-s.jpg)

### Reliable connectivity, even when the grid fails

Upgrade your Wi-Fi connection to Cellular for a more reliable monitoring experience. With automatic carrier switching and the ability to monitor during outages, the [Connectivity Cellular Accessory](https://www.generacpowerproducts.com/Parts/Connectivity/Generac-Connectivity-Cellular-Accessory/p/G0072150 "click or tap to open") is the key to unlocking next level generator monitoring.

![](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/home-standby-generators/generac_37186mobilelinkscreen-s.jpg)

### Continuous protection starts with a full tank

A critical part of maintaining your generator is making sure there is enough fuel to get you through unexpected power outages. Our optional [Generac Fuel Monitor](https://generac.ordertree.com/lte-kit-tank-monitor/5638603678.p "click or tap to open") integrates with Mobile Link to provide tank level readings, offering customers the ultimate peace of mind.

![An image of a Generac fuel monitor and the mobile link app](https://www.generac.com/globalassets/residential/home-standby-generators/home-standby-generators/mobile-link/fuel-monitor.png)

### additional mobile link accessories

Mobile Link offers a variety of monitoring upgrades that install directly to your home standby generator. Visit our [accessories page](https://www.generac.com/home-standby-generators/wifi-generator-monitoring/accessory-products/) to learn which is best for you.
