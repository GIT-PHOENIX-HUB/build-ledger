# Generator Sizing Guide -- Residential

**Source:** Compiled from Generac sizing resources, scraped 2026-04-14.

## How to Size a Home Standby Generator

Sizing a home standby generator requires calculating the total wattage of
the circuits and appliances the homeowner wants to back up during an outage.

### Step-by-Step Sizing Process

1. **List essential circuits** -- Identify which circuits the homeowner needs
   during an outage (lighting, refrigerator, HVAC, well pump, medical equipment).
2. **Add up running watts** -- Total the running wattage of all selected loads.
3. **Account for starting watts** -- Motors (AC compressors, well pumps, sump
   pumps) draw 3-5x their running watts on startup. Use the largest motor's
   starting surge, not all of them simultaneously.
4. **Add a safety margin** -- Add 10-20% above calculated load.
5. **Match to generator size** -- Select the smallest generator that exceeds
   the total calculated load.

### Common Load Estimates

| Appliance | Running Watts | Starting Watts |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| Central AC (3-ton) | 3,800 | 11,400 |
| Central AC (5-ton) | 6,200 | 18,600 |
| Electric Range | 5,000 | 5,000 |
| Electric Dryer | 5,400 | 5,400 |
| Electric Water Heater | 4,500 | 4,500 |
| Refrigerator | 700 | 2,100 |
| Well Pump (1 HP) | 1,000 | 3,000 |
| Sump Pump (1/2 HP) | 800 | 2,400 |
| Furnace Blower | 800 | 2,400 |
| Lighting (per circuit) | 600 | 600 |
| Garage Door Opener | 720 | 1,440 |

### Quick Sizing Rules of Thumb

| Home Size | Recommended Generator | Coverage |
|-----------|----------------------|----------|
| < 1,500 sq ft | 10kW | Essential circuits |
| 1,500 - 2,500 sq ft | 14-18kW | Most circuits + small AC |
| 2,500 - 3,500 sq ft | 22kW | Whole house with central AC |
| 3,500 - 5,000 sq ft | 24-26kW | Whole house with large AC |
| 5,000+ sq ft | 26-28kW | Large whole house |

### Important Notes for Phoenix Electric Technicians

- Always perform a load calculation on-site, not just a square footage estimate.
- Check the main panel amperage (100A, 150A, 200A) -- this affects transfer switch selection.
- Natural gas ratings are approximately 10-15% lower than LP ratings.
- If the home has a tankless electric water heater, that can add 18-27kW alone --
  these homes may need liquid-cooled units (not currently in our lineup).
