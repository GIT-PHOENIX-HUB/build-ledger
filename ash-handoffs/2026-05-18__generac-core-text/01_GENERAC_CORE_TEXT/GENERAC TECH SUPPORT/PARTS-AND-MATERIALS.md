## Oil Specifications

- **Recommended:** Generac 5W-20 full synthetic — good for ALL temperature ranges
- **Alternative (older units):** 5W-30 synthetic (check owner's manual)
- **DO NOT use conventional oil**

### Oil Capacity (with filter)

| Model Size | Guardian | Next Gen |
|---|---|---|
| 10 kW | ~1.1 qt (1.03 L) | ~1.4 qt (1.3 L) |
| 14/18 kW | ~2.2 qt (2.1 L) | ~2.5 qt (2.4 L) |
| 20–26 kW | ~1.9 qt (1.8 L) | ~2.2 qt (2.1 L) |
| 22–28 kW | — | ~2.2 qt (2.1 L) |

## Battery Specifications

| Spec | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Type | 12V lead-acid or AGM |
| Group | 26R (standard) or 35 (AGM option) |
| CCA (minimum) | 540 CCA (Group 26R) / 650 CCA (Group 35 AGM) |
| Voltage (healthy, charged) | 12.6V+ |
| Replace when | Voltage consistently below 12.1V after full charge, or fails load test |

**Battery is NOT included with the generator — purchased separately.**

---

## Spark Plugs

| Engine | Spark Plug Count | Replace Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Single cylinder (410cc, 530cc) | 1 | Schedule B (4yr / 400hr) |
| Dual cylinder (760cc, 816cc, 992cc, 999cc) | 2 | Schedule B (4yr / 400hr) |

Spark plugs are included in maintenance kits. Gap per owner's manual for specific engine.

---

## Air Filter

- Included in all maintenance kits
- Replace at Schedule B (4yr/400hr) minimum
- Inspect annually — replace if dirty or damaged
- Verify air box is sealed after reinstall

---

## Oil Filter

- Included in all maintenance kits
- Replace at every oil change
- Hand-tighten until gasket contacts adapter, then tighten 3/4 to 1 full turn
- Apply thin coat of clean oil to new filter gasket before install

---

## Fuses

| Fuse | Location | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 7.5A ATO fuse | Controller (under rubber USB port flap on Evolution) | Protects controller circuit |
| ATS fuses (x3) | Inside automatic transfer switch | Protect 194/23/N wires between gen and ATS |

**CAUTION:** T1 fuse is ALWAYS under 120V even when generator is off.

## Fuel System Components

### Internal Fuel Regulator
- **Guardian series:** Demand-type regulator — provides fuel as engine demands it based on load
- **Next Gen series:** Dual shutoff valve with ECM (engine control module)
- Located behind right side panel
- Part number: Use generac.ordertree.com → enter model/serial → find in exploded view

### External Fuel Regulator
- NOT part of the generator system
- NOT covered under warranty
- Purchase from Generac dealer, licensed plumber, or gas provider
- Required in certain installations where fuel pressure needs control at source

### Fuel Selector Knob (Guardian)
- Orange knob on mixer — set to LP or NG
- **If wrong fuel type is selected, unit will not start or will run rough**

### Fuel Conversion (Next Gen)
- No physical fuel selector knob
- Fuel type set via **Field Pro app**

## Transfer Switch Components

### ATS Models
| Rating | Model Numbers | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 100A | RXSW100A3SPD | SE and Non-SE |
| 200A | RXSW200A3SPD | SE and Non-SE |

### ATS Contents (from loose parts bag)
- Owner's manual
- Warranty statement
- Manual operating handle
- Storage bracket for manual operating handle
- Fault current label
- Clear decal for fault current label

### ATS Control Wires
| Wire | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 194 | Provides 12 VDC to ATS — always powered |
| 23 | Signal ground — controlled by generator control panel, grounded when gen starts after power loss |
| T1 | 120V to battery charger — **ALWAYS HOT** |
| N1 | Neutral |

### Load Management Options
- **SACM (Smart A/C Module):** Controls A/C load during generator operation
- **SMM (Smart Management Module):** Sold separately, additional load shedding
